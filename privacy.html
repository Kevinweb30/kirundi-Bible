Privacy Policy

Bibiliya Yera · last updated October 2026

Bibiliya Yera is a free Bible reading and listening app for the Kirundi language. This page explains, plainly, what the app does and does not do with your information.

We don't collect personal data

The app does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email, location, or any other personal information, and does not include analytics or advertising trackers.

What stays on your device

Bookmarks, highlights, personal notes, your last-read chapter and listening position, your reading-plan progress, your playback speed, and any chapters you choose to download for offline listening are all stored only in your own device's local storage. This information is never sent to us or to any server — we simply have no access to it. If you delete the app or clear its data, this information is gone.

Audio and text content

Bible text and audio are loaded from our hosting (GitHub Pages) as you read and listen. Standard web server logs (such as IP address and request time) may be recorded by our hosting provider for security and reliability purposes, in line with GitHub's own privacy statement. We do not separately collect or review this information.

Daily verse reminders (app only)

If you install the Bibiliya Yera app and allow notifications, the app schedules a daily verse reminder directly on your device (around 7:00 AM). These reminders are created and delivered by your phone itself: no account, push token, or personal information is sent to us or to any notification service. You can turn the reminder off at any time from the app's menu or in your device's Settings. Nothing changes for people using the website instead of the app.
