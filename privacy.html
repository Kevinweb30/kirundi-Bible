Privacy Policy

Bibiliya Yera · last updated October 2026

Bibiliya Yera is a free Bible reading and listening app for the Kirundi language. This page explains, plainly, what the app does and does not do with your information.

We don't collect personal data

The app does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email, location, or any other personal information, and does not include analytics or advertising trackers.

What stays on your device

Bookmarks, highlights, personal notes, your last-read chapter, and any chapters you choose to download for offline listening are all stored only in your own device's local storage. This information is never sent to us or to any server — we simply have no access to it. If you delete the app or clear its data, this information is gone.

Audio and text content

Bible text and audio are loaded from our hosting (GitHub Pages) as you read and listen. Standard web server logs (such as IP address and request time) may be recorded by our hosting provider for security and reliability purposes, in line with GitHub's own privacy statement. We do not separately collect or review this information.

Daily verse notifications (app only)

If you install the Bibiliya Yera app and allow notifications, your device is given an anonymous push token so we can deliver a daily verse notification. This token does not include your name or any personal information, and is used only for sending that notification through our delivery service, OneSignal — see OneSignal's privacy policy. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device's Settings, which stops this immediately. Nothing changes for people using the website instead of the app.

Changes
