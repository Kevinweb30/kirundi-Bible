French Bible text for Bibiliya Yera
-----------------------------------
Text: J. N. Darby (traduction française, "La Bible"), public domain.
Prepared for the reader: one file per book (books/CODE.json). Chapters and verses follow the
KIRUNDI Bible's numbering, so each verse lines up with the Kirundi audio. Where the Darby
numbering differs (Psalm titles, Joel, Malachi, a few verses elsewhere), the text was matched to
the Kirundi verse it belongs to; a verse the Darby edition does not have shows as "—".
Footnote letters, footnotes and the editorial marks were removed.
  books/CODE.json    {"chapters": {"1": {"v": ["verse 1", ...], "t": "Psalm title (optional)"}}}
  search-index.json  one row per verse, for searching
  daily-verses.json  the verse-of-the-day list (same verses as ../daily-verses.json)
