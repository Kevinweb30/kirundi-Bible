English Bible text for Bibiliya Yera
------------------------------------
Text: Berean Standard Bible (BSB), public domain (placed in the public domain on April 30, 2023
by the Berean Bible Translation Committee, berean.bible).
Prepared for the reader: one file per book (books/CODE.json). Chapters and verses follow the
KIRUNDI Bible's numbering, so each verse lines up with the Kirundi audio. A verse that the BSB
does not have (it follows the critical Greek text; 16 verses in the Gospels, Acts and Romans)
shows as "—". In 3 John the last verse of the BSB was split into the two Kirundi verses 14 and 15.
Footnote markers and footnotes were removed. Section headings and Psalm titles are kept.
  books/CODE.json    {"chapters": {"1": {"v": ["verse 1", ...], "h": [[verse, "heading", 1 = psalm title], ...]}}}
  search-index.json  one row per verse, for searching
  daily-verses.json  the verse-of-the-day list (same verses as ../daily-verses.json)
